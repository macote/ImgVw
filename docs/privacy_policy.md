# ImgVw Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** July 10, 2026

ImgVw is an offline image viewer for Windows developed and published by Marc-André Côté. This privacy policy explains
how ImgVw handles information when you use the application.

## Summary

ImgVw does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. It does not contain advertising, analytics,
telemetry, user accounts, or cloud services. Images opened in ImgVw are processed locally on your device.

## Information handled on your device

To provide its image-viewing features, ImgVw may access and process the following information locally:

- image files that you open or make accessible to the application;
- image contents and embedded metadata needed to decode and display those files, such as dimensions, color profiles,
  and EXIF orientation;
- file and folder names, paths, sizes, and other filesystem information needed to browse supported images in the same
  folder or in folders you ask ImgVw to search;
- a custom ICC color profile if you choose one;
- application settings and temporary decoded-image data needed while the application is running.

This information remains on your device. ImgVw does not send it to the developer or to any third party.

## Local storage and deletion

ImgVw may store application settings and a user-selected ICC color profile in application data managed by Windows.
Temporary image data may be written to the Windows temporary-files location to support image decoding and caching.
ImgVw removes its temporary files when they are no longer needed during normal operation, although files can remain
after an abnormal termination and may later be removed by Windows or the user.

When installed through the Microsoft Store, Windows manages installation, updates, and removal of the application.
Uninstalling ImgVw removes package-managed application data according to Windows behavior. Portable or separately
installed versions may leave user settings or a selected ICC profile in the user's application-data folder.

ImgVw can delete or recycle an image only when you invoke the corresponding command. Those operations affect the image
file on your device and are handled through Windows; they do not send the file anywhere.

## Network access and third parties

ImgVw does not use an internet connection and does not include third-party analytics or advertising services.

The Microsoft Store and Windows may independently process information related to acquiring, installing, updating,
licensing, securing, or diagnosing applications. That processing is controlled by Microsoft and is subject to
[Microsoft's Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement), not this policy.

## Children's privacy

ImgVw does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users. The application displays
only image files that the user or operating system makes available to it.

## Security

Because ImgVw processes files locally, you remain responsible for controlling access to images and other files on your
device. Keep Windows and ImgVw up to date and obtain ImgVw from the Microsoft Store or the project's official release
page.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if ImgVw's features or information-handling practices change. Material changes will be
published with a new effective date. The policy applicable to a release will remain available in the ImgVw source
repository.

## Contact

For privacy questions or concerns, contact the ImgVw developer by opening an issue in the
[ImgVw GitHub repository](https://github.com/macote/ImgVw/issues). Do not include private images, sensitive file paths,
or other confidential information in a public issue.
